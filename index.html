About

At Kling AI, my research focuses on video generation model pretraining.

My broader research interests include efficient visual generation, dynamic neural networks, and efficient inference for visual and language models. My work spans image and video diffusion, world simulation, and agentic model routing. I also study long-tailed learning and fine-grained learning.
